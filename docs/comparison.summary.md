# A subagent vs a SpecAgent role session: summary for the owner

- Scope first: hard work of many packages, days and roles; a short lookup inside one turn stays a subagent's job.
- A table of 15 properties (context, memory, observability, resumability, isolation, verification, tests, feedback
  loop, cycles to a product, cost, cache, parallelism, failure, scale, knowledge), each row with its evidence, and
  seven things a role session can do and a subagent cannot.
- The measured numbers of 2026-10-30 (98% cache read, 103 sessions in one run, a spec written, built, verified and
  merged in one run of 3 h 12 min, fixes minutes after the unit, tests with every fix) and ADR-0006's one-task
  measurement stated as the ADR reads it.
