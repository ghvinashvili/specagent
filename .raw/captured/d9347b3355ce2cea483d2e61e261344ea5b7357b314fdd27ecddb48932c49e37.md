# ECC content is pinned, never its mechanism

The template keeps copies of chosen ECC files at one pinned commit with their licence and checksums, the rules under
`.claude/rules/ecc/`, the skills under `.claude/skills/`, the agent texts as brief material under
`docs/references/ecc-agents/` (never `.claude/agents/`); a re-pin follows `docs/references/ecc-sources.md`.
