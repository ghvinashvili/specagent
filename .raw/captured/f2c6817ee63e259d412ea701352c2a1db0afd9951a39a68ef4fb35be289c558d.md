# The manager's profile is measured at its first call

The manager starts under 30,000 tokens, the owner's requirement for its own context; on 2026-10-29 its first call
measured 51,915 tokens with the desktop app's 36 plugins. Two profiles can run it: a terminal `claude` profile with
three or four plugins and MCP servers loaded on need, or the template's `just manager` agent profile (`justfile`). On
2026-10-29 at 17:03 UTC the owner answered question 5 of the manager spec with its recommendation: both profiles are
measured once at their first call and the one under 30,000 tokens is kept; neither is chosen beforehand.

The template ships the measurement. `scripts/manager/measure_manager.py first-call` takes one transcript of each
profile and prints each first call (its input, cache write and cache read tokens) with `pass` under the limit
(`--limit`, 30,000 by default) and `fail` at or over it; once a profile is kept, it runs on three starts in a row. The
`## Measuring itself` section of `docs/roles/manager-chat.md` gives the order, and the skill's
`scripts/manager/README.md` and `references/measuring.md` describe the script beside the others.
