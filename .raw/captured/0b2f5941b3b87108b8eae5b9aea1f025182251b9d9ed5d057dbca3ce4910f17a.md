# No fresh session after a finished role's hand-off

The watcher starts a fresh session on a hand-off note only when the role has work to take. An agent of the config may
carry a `work_check`: a command, run in the run folder with `{run}` and `{skill}` filled in, that reads the same signal
as the role's own waiter, run once. Exit 0 (work waits) launches the fresh session as before. Exit 3 (nothing to take)
logs `idle <agent>: nothing to take (<last line>)`, keeps the note as the role's `idle` and launches nothing; from then
on the role's whole turn in each round is its check, with no revival, nudge or timeout launch, and the watcher launches
`fresh <agent> after idle: work waits` once the check passes. Any other exit, or a check that cannot run, launches as
before, so a broken check never silences a role. A note that answers the watcher's own hand-over order (the cap's or
the unit end's, which may come in the middle of a unit) launches without the check. A config reload and the registry
keep the idle state, so a restarted watcher re-checks the role instead of taking its note as new.

The example config gives a check to the two roles whose hand-overs can leave nothing to take: the critic
(`wait_for_task.py --log … --once`: a written task not yet reviewed, or the summary due) and the plan fixer
(`open_ids.py {run} --kind C,A,B,V,R --cause plan`: the ids its brief collects, no `(code)` V or R). The builders and
the verifier hand over after a unit by design and get none.
