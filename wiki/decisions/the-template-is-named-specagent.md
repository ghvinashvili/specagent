# The template is named SpecAgent

The template is named SpecAgent, written as one word and read as "special agent". Its text and the names of its own things
use it, and the former name is retired. The repository is renamed `specagent` at the public push, and the old address
redirects. The commons folder `~/aftpro-commons` and the marketplace name `example-repo-plugins` keep their names.
