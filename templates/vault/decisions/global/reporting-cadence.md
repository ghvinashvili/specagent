# Reporting cadence

**Rule.** The kind of work sets the cadence of reports. A batch of work is finished before the reply, with one report at the end; a test or measurement running more than about a minute reports every minute; a search for a working idea reports at the end, with one line per failure at most.

**Why.** A batch asked for three times arrived a third at a time, and each new backlog reported as a pause wore the owner out (2026-09-11). A silent three-minute test drew an unhappy reply (2026-09-24), and after many loss reports the owner no longer wanted them while the search went on.

**How to apply.**
- *A batch* ("do all of them", "finish it") is an instruction about the turn as well as the work: do every item, commit as you go so nothing is lost, write one report at the end. Interim replies are only for what needs the owner's decision. Too large for one stretch: say so once, at the start.
- Work found mid-task is absorbed, not handed over: state the total scope in one sentence, say you are continuing, continue; never present new items as a menu, a decision or a reason to pause. One-line "what I am doing now" lines between big tool calls are fine ([[answers-short-plain-bottom-line-first]]); stopping the turn to report and ask is not.
- *A test running more than about a minute* goes to the background with its log on disk; every minute, one or two lines: what happened so far, any errors. Before starting, say in one line which errors are expected.
- *A search* moves from one failed idea to the next without presenting each failure. Failures go to a register file (each idea written down before it runs, its result appended, the count of ideas tried carried forward).
- Come back with a full report when something passes the owner's own test, or when the next step is genuinely the owner's (data to buy, overnight holding, a different vehicle, accepting market exposure as a strategy).
- Honesty does not relax: blind tests, no look-ahead, a lucky-looking result called what it is, the count of ideas tried with every result.
