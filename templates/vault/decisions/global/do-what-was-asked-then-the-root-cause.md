# Do what was asked, then the root cause

**Rule.** Do what was asked and nothing beside it. A negative result is not a deliverable: it comes with its cause, the strongest honest version of the idea, and a root fix.

**Why.** An honest negative reported as the day's result drew the owner's question whether it was not being fixed, and a test built into the live runner drew the order not to take decisions on my own. A negative on a voting panel was not accepted: rebuilt in its strongest form, the panel turned a loss into a gain. Cosmetic choices listed as decisions for the owner drew the question whether only simple solutions were being offered.

**How to apply.**
- A requested test or measurement is produced and stopped at: it asks for numbers, not for a change to the system. The live path, defaults, frozen rules and running strategies (even on paper) stay as they are until the owner says otherwise; nothing new is enabled by default.
- A number the owner gives is the setting, not a ceiling to beat; an unclear number or timing gets one short question before the run; their word for stop means stop everything and wait. If the measurement is impossible with the data at hand, say so in one or two sentences with what it would take; no adjacent work is substituted.
- A negative result is not a deliverable: the same message says its cause and what is being done about it. Refusing to tune a parameter until it looks good is not refusing to fix; find the defect or the rule the system does not follow.
- Before calling an idea dead, build its strongest honest form once, measure that and name the form; when the owner asks for better, strengthen the mechanism, not abandon it. Split-half and held-out discipline still apply: strengthening is no licence to search until something passes. If nothing is fixable without inventing a rule, say so and name what would settle it.
- Fixes are structural: before offering a choice, ask whether the problem is a symptom upstream (engine, data shape, structure) and propose that root fix; a label, toggle or partial rule is not offered where the real fix belongs elsewhere. Only questions that are genuinely the owner's (how it looks, what the product does) go to them.
- Inside the asked deliverable, finding and fixing the cause is part of the job; beyond it (what runs, what is on by default, what is frozen) nothing changes without the owner's word.
