# Status and decisions

**Rule.** When the owner asks where things stand, answer with the current step and the next one or two in two or three short lines (`now: …, next: …`); costs and the long plan only when asked. When several chats work together, the coordinating chat asks the owner's decisions, one at a time, each with a recommendation; a side chat prepares the list. Never open a question popup (`AskUserQuestion`) for a decision unless the owner asks for one: ask in plain text, at most once, and do not repeat the question.

**Why.** The owner follows from a phone and wants answers at a glance: a status overview in four sections was answered with a request to be brief. For the popup the owner gave no reason; one place to answer is the likely one, since a popup was denied without a word and the decisions were then answered in the coordinating chat.

**How to apply.**
- Status: the current step and the next one or two, in two or three short lines.
- Decisions: asked by the coordinating chat only, one at a time, each with a recommendation.
- Ask in plain text, once; no popup unless the owner asks for one.
