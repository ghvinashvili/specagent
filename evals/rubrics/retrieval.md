# Rubric: an answer from the knowledge layer is grounded

Grade each dimension from 0 to 1. Answer "Unknown" for a dimension the material does not let you judge.

1. Faithfulness: every statement of the answer is supported by a passage the answer cites.
2. Answer relevance: the answer addresses the question that was asked and nothing else.
3. Context precision: the pages and passages the answer cites were needed for it.
4. Context recall: no passage of the fixture that the reference answer depends on is missing from the citations.

Score = mean of the four dimensions; "Unknown" is excluded from the mean and reported.
