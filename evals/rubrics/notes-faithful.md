# Rubric: notes are faithful to the source

Grade each dimension separately from 0 to 1. Answer "Unknown" for a dimension you cannot judge from the material.

1. Every quoted sentence appears verbatim in the source file at the cited line.
2. No statement in the notes goes beyond what the source says.
3. Every open question in questions.md is genuinely unanswered by the source.

Score = mean of the three dimensions; "Unknown" is excluded from the mean and reported.
