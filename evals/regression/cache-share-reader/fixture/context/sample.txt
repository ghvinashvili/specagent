Retry policy for the upload client.
A failed upload is retried at most three times.
The wait before the first retry is two seconds.
Each later wait is twice the previous one.
A response with status 400 is never retried.
A response with status 503 is always retried.
After the last retry fails, the file name is written to failed.log.
The policy was introduced in version 1.4.
Uploads larger than 2 GB are split before any retry logic applies.
